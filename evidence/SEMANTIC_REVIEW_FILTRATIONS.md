# Filtrations and Decidability: source and semantic review

Scope: frozen OpenLogic OLP-0450–OLP-0459, the chapter driver and nine Filtrations sections, translated into India-standard Bengali. The manifest and per-file source hashes identify the frozen source; the Bengali files preserve chapter imports, section IDs, theorem labels, tagged Box/Diamond branches, TikZ diagrams, tables, and formal environments. Structural verification covers 103 aligned blocks, 89 translated blocks and 14 unchanged formal or structural blocks.

The India-standard Bengali canon consulted for this tranche comprises the Tripura mathematical-reasoning/connectives page BN-IN-P008 (printed page 153), the Suri Vidyasagar College proposition and quantification page BN-IN-P009 (printed pages 110–111), and the NSOU relation and proof pages BN-IN-P013 and BN-IN-P025 (printed pages 360 and 368). Their page images were visually reread on 2026-09-26 and their source/image hashes are checked by the segment indexer. They support the surrounding mathematical register, relation properties, proof and injective-function language. They do not directly attest the specialist words “filtration,” “finite model property,” or “modal closure”; T338–T343 remain definition-governed and open to expert correction.

Semantic review, by source unit:

- OLP-0450 preserves all nine imports and the chapter identity.
- OLP-0451 distinguishes effective validity testing from one-sided finite-countermodel search. For a fixed n-world frame, the 2^(nm) valuations and 2^(n²) relations are kept distinct; the formula-dependent finite-model bound supplies termination. The quotient-world construction preserves truth at [w]. BN-SRC-360 repairs the frozen illustration's false existential Box clause by explicitly taking a universal relation and requiring B at every world; BN-SRC-361 restores V*(p) in its atomic base case.
- OLP-0452 retains subformula closure, the stronger modal closure condition, equivalence by agreement on Γ, reflexivity/symmetry/transitivity and the partition argument.
- OLP-0453 keeps all three filtration conditions: inherited R edges; the Box preservation obligation; and the Diamond preservation obligation. Every active tagged induction case retains its direction, and the theorem, exercise and class-validity corollary remain separate.
- OLP-0454 distinguishes the existential-edge finest filtration from the constraint-defined coarsest one. Both proof branches, the positive-integer successor model, its two possible quotient relations, the binary-tree diagram and the concluding exercise are preserved.
- OLP-0455 retains the injection from quotient classes W* into the power set of Γ and the bound |W*| ≤ 2^n. This is a bound on worlds, not on the total number of finite models.
- OLP-0456 keeps the K argument, universal-model equivalence, S5 finite-model-property consequence and the two property-preservation exercises. BN-SRC-362 restores [w] as the world of the filtered model.
- OLP-0457 keeps the two parallel enumerations, proof search and countermodel search. BN-SRC-365 restricts the latter to finite universal models; otherwise a non-universal countermodel could falsely refute an S5 theorem.
- OLP-0458 retains all four C_i conditions, their Box/Diamond tagged table rows, all four preservation cases and the worked symmetric case.
- OLP-0459 retains both serial Euclidean diagrams and the distinction between arbitrary subformula-closed Γ and infinite modally closed Γ. BN-SRC-363 expresses required new arrows between quotient classes; BN-SRC-364 aligns the three proof items with the theorem's symmetry, transitivity and Euclidean order, leaving the transitivity argument and the two exercises intact. The modal-closure construction alone makes no claim of a finite-model property.
